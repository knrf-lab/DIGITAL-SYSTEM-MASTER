No. 21 vs 22: \(\overline{\bar x + \bar x} = \bar{\bar x} = x\) (karena \(\bar x+\bar x=\bar x\)), tetapi \(\overline{x + \bar x} = \bar 1 = 0\). Beda tipis!
\(\overline{ab} \ne \bar a\,\bar b\). Bar panjang ≠ bar pendek. Cek dengan a = 1, b = 0: \(\overline{ab} = 1\), tapi \(\bar a\bar b = 0\).
